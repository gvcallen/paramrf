GeneralizedLine

class pmrf.models.components.lines.empirical.GeneralizedLine(length: Any, zn: Any = 50.0, ep_eff: Any = 1.0, A: Any = 0.0, tand: Any = 0.0, f_ref: Any = 1000000000.0, f_low: Any = 1000.0, f_high: Any = 1000000000000.0, R_dc: Any = 0.0, f_s: Any = None, conductor_terms: Any = (), dielectric_terms: Any = (), inductive_dispersion: Any = (), capacitive_dispersion: Any = (), *, name: str | None = None, metadata: Any = None)

Bases: AbstractEffectiveLine

Causal effective-value line with RL, RC and LC refinement terms.

The base is the first-order effective-value line, as PhysicalLine with FirstOrderEffectiveLineFormulation. On top of it, an optional skin-effect onset, a DC resistance and four families of terms model higher-order behaviour: extra conductor relaxations (RLTerm), dielectric relaxations (RCTerm) and modal dispersion of the inductance and capacitance (LCTerm). Every part is causal, so the line is too. With R_dc = 0, f_s = None and no terms it is exactly the first-order PhysicalLine.

Mathematical Formulation

With the nominal \(R'_n(\omega)\), \(L'_n\) and \(C'_n\) of EffectiveValues,

\[Z'(\omega) = j\omega L'_n \mu(\omega) + Z'_{int}(\omega) \qquad Y'(\omega) = j\omega C'_n \frac{\varepsilon(\omega)}{\varepsilon_{eff}}.\]

The medium \((\varepsilon, \mu)\) is a PoleDielectric whose base is the DjordjevicSarkarDielectric with \(\varepsilon_r' = \varepsilon_{eff}\), \(\tan\delta\), f_low, f_high and f_ref. Each RCTerm adds a DebyePole with \(\Delta\varepsilon_r = 2\varepsilon_{eff}\tan\delta_i\) and \(f_{relax} = f_i\), each capacitive LCTerm a LorentzPole with \(\Delta\varepsilon_r = \varepsilon_{eff} a_i\), and each inductive LCTerm a permeability LorentzPole with \(\Delta\mu_r = a_i\), both at \(f_{res} = f_i\) with damping \(\zeta_i\). So an RC term adds

\[j\omega C'_n \frac{2\tan\delta_i}{1 + j\omega/\omega_i}\]
to \(Y'\), and an undamped LC term adds
\[j\omega C'_n \frac{a_i \omega_i^2}{\omega_i^2 - \omega^2} \quad\text{or}\quad j\omega L'_n \frac{a_i \omega_i^2}{\omega_i^2 - \omega^2}\]
to \(Y'\) or \(Z'\). A damped one, \(\zeta_i > 0\), is a lossy RLC term.

The conductor’s internal impedance is

\[Z'_{int}(\omega) = R_{dc} + \frac{2}{\pi}(1 + j) R'_n(\omega) \arctan\sqrt{\frac{j\omega}{\omega_s}} + 2 z_n \frac{\ln 10}{20} \sum_i A_i \frac{j\omega}{\omega_i + j\omega},\]
where the sum runs over conductor_terms, each a parallel \(R_i \parallel L_i\) with \(R_i = 2 z_n \frac{\ln 10}{20} A_i\) and \(L_i = R_i/\omega_i\). The arctan is the closed form of a distribution of RL terms truncated below \(\omega_s\),
\[\frac{\sqrt{2} R'_n(\omega_{ref})}{\pi\sqrt{\omega_{ref}}} \int_{\omega_s}^{\infty} \frac{j\omega}{\sqrt{x}(x + j\omega)}\,dx,\]
which tends to \((1 + j) R'_n(\omega)\) as \(\omega_s \to 0\). With f_s = None the onset is absent and that limit is used exactly.

ep_eff and tand describe the base medium at f_ref. The terms add to it, so once any are present \(\Re\,\varepsilon(f_{ref}) \neq \varepsilon_{eff}\). Each RC term also raises the static capacitance by \(2\tan\delta_i C'_n\), as the Kramers-Kronig relations require. An undamped LC term is singular at its resonance, so f must lie outside the evaluated band; LorentzPole raises if it does not.

Example

import pmrf as prf
from pmrf.models import GeneralizedLine, LCTerm

line = GeneralizedLine(
    zn=50.0,
    ep_eff=2.1,
    A=0.05,
    tand=2e-4,
    length=1.0,
    f_s=1e6,
    conductor_terms=[(0.01, 10e6)],
    dielectric_terms=[(1e-4, 100e6)],
    capacitive_dispersion=[LCTerm(a=1e-3, f=20e9)],
)

freq = prf.Frequency(start=1, stop=1000, npoints=101, unit='mhz')
s = line.s(freq)

Parameters:

  • zn (Param, default=50.0) – Nominal characteristic impedance in ohm, defining the L/C ratio.

  • ep_eff (Param, default=1.0) – Effective relative permittivity of the base medium at f_ref.

  • A (Param, default=0.0) – Conductor attenuation in dB/m at f_ref.

  • tand (Param, default=0.0) – Effective loss tangent of the base medium at f_ref.

  • f_ref (Param, default=1e9) – Reference frequency in Hz of ep_eff, A and tand.

  • f_low (Param, default=1e3) – Lower bound of the base medium’s relaxation-time distribution in Hz.

  • f_high (Param, default=1e12) – Upper bound of the base medium’s relaxation-time distribution in Hz.

  • R_dc (Param, default=0.0) – DC series resistance in ohm/m.

  • f_s (Param | None, default=None) – Skin-effect onset frequency in Hz, or None for no onset.

  • conductor_terms (tuple of RLTerm, default=()) – Conductor relaxations. (A, f) tuples are coerced to RLTerm.

  • dielectric_terms (tuple of RCTerm, default=()) – Dielectric relaxations. (tand, f) tuples are coerced to RCTerm.

  • inductive_dispersion (tuple of LCTerm, default=()) – Resonances in the inductance. (a, f) or (a, f, zeta) tuples are coerced to LCTerm.

  • capacitive_dispersion (tuple of LCTerm, default=()) – Resonances in the capacitance, coerced as inductive_dispersion.
