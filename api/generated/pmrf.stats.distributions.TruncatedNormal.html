TruncatedNormal

class pmrf.stats.distributions.TruncatedNormal(loc: Array, scale: Array, low: Array, high: Array)

Bases: AbstractProbDistribution

Truncated Normal distribution with loc, scale, low, and high parameters.

Initializes a Truncated Normal distribution.

Arguments:

  • loc: Mean of the untruncated distribution.

  • scale: Standard deviation of the untruncated distribution.

  • low: Lower bound of the truncation.

  • high: Upper bound of the truncation.
