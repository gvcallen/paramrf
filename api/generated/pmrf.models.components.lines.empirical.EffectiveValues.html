EffectiveValues

class pmrf.models.components.lines.empirical.EffectiveValues(zn: Array, ep_eff: Array, A: Array, tand: Array, f_ref: Array)

Bases: Module

The effective values that describe an effective-value line.

Every effective-value line reduces to these five numbers.

Mathematical Formulation

The attenuation constants are

\[\alpha_c(\omega) = \frac{\ln 10}{20} A \sqrt{\frac{\omega}{\omega_{ref}}} \qquad \alpha_d(\omega) = \frac{\omega \sqrt{\varepsilon_{eff}} \tan\delta}{2 c_0}\]
in Np/m, and the nominal per-unit-length quantities
\[R'_n = 2 z_n \alpha_c \qquad L'_n = \frac{z_n \sqrt{\varepsilon_{eff}}}{c_0} \qquad G'_n = \frac{2 \alpha_d}{z_n} \qquad C'_n = \frac{\sqrt{\varepsilon_{eff}}}{z_n c_0}.\]

References

Pozar, D. M. (2011). Microwave Engineering (4th ed.), Section 2.7. Wiley.

Parameters:

  • zn (ArrayLike) – Nominal characteristic impedance in ohm.

  • ep_eff (ArrayLike) – Effective relative permittivity at f_ref.

  • A (ArrayLike) – Conductor attenuation in dB/m at f_ref.

  • tand (ArrayLike) – Effective loss tangent at f_ref.

  • f_ref (ArrayLike) – Reference frequency in Hz.
